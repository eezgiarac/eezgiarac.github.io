# Unit 1: Introduction and GitHub Setup

## Unit Activities

During this unit, I reviewed the guidance for setting up GitHub and using GitHub Copilot. I also created a repository to organise my weekly activities and record the development of my e-portfolio.

The collaborative discussion examined the use of AI-powered assistants in business operations. The activity considered examples involving tools developed by OpenAI, Google and Microsoft, as well as the use of artificial intelligence by organisations such as PwC and Amazon.

The discussion was guided by the following questions:

- How does AI-powered automation transform businesses?
- What ethical concerns arise from adopting AI in business operations?
- How do AI, cybersecurity and software engineering contribute to AI-powered assistants?

## My Discussion Post

For my initial contribution, I chose Instagram as an example of a business platform that uses artificial intelligence.

My post examined:

- AI-powered recommendation systems;
- personalised advertising and content discovery;
- generative images and misinformation;
- algorithmic influence on trust and content diversity;
- the limitations of labelling AI-generated content.

[Read my initial discussion post](artificial-intelligence-on-instagram.md)

## Reflection

The activity helped me connect several computing disciplines within one practical example. AI and data science support Instagram’s recommendation systems, while software engineering enables these systems to operate at scale. Cybersecurity is relevant because the platform processes large amounts of personal and behavioural data and must address threats involving privacy, manipulated content and account security.

The sources did not all serve the same purpose. The academic studies provided evidence about social media, misinformation and user trust. Meta’s announcement explained the company’s proposed labelling system, but it represented the platform’s own account of its response. It therefore could not independently demonstrate how effective the system would be in practice.

This comparison helped me understand why company announcements should be considered alongside independent academic research.
