
# Artificial Intelligence in Enterprise Cybersecurity

## Project Overview
This project explores how artificial intelligence can support enterprise cybersecurity while introducing new security, privacy and ethical challenges.

The presentation examines AI-assisted threat detection, incident response, human oversight and responsible AI governance.

## Key Topics
- AI applications in enterprise cybersecurity
- Threat detection and security monitoring
- Cybersecurity risks and limitations of AI
- Data privacy and ethical considerations
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- GDPR and the EU AI Act
- Critical evaluation of AI tools
